3. Potential issues while filing ITR -1
ITR -1 is simple form. But there could be some potential issues may come. Those are discussed here and in the table.
When a conflict is identified between the primary data source and the validation source, the product must not make an
assumption or silently pick one value over the other. The following process applies:
3.1 What the Product Must Do When a Conflict is Detected
•     Pause the population of the affected field.
•    Show the taxpayer both figures — the value extracted from the primary source (e.g., Form 16 gross salary) and the
value from the validation source (e.g., AIS salary data) — with a plain-language explanation of why they differ.
•    Ask the taxpayer a specific, plain-language question requesting confirmation or a supporting document. Do not ask
vague questions — the question must name the exact figures and the exact discrepancy.
•    Accept the taxpayer's response — either a confirmed figure with a reason, or an uploaded supporting document —
and update the field accordingly.
•    Re-run all affected validation checks before proceeding to the next section.

5. Key Product Behaviour Requirements
5.1 ITR-1 Eligibility Check
Before the product begins collecting any tax information, it must verify that the taxpayer is eligible to file
ITR-1. If any disqualifying condition is identified — total income above ₹50 lakhs, business income of
any kind, more than one house property, LTCG above ₹1.25 lakhs, directorship, unlisted equity, non-
resident status, or agricultural income above ₹5,000 — the product must redirect the taxpayer to the
appropriate form (ITR-2, ITR-3, or ITR-4) without allowing the ITR-1 filing to proceed.
5.2 Tax Regime Selection
The product must calculate the taxpayer's tax liability under both the Old Tax Regime and the New Tax
Regime before asking the taxpayer to choose. The comparison must be shown in plain language. The
taxpayer must actively confirm their choice. Once a regime is selected, the product must enforce all
applicable rules for that regime throughout the return — including blocking deductions that are not
permitted under the selected regime. The regime cannot be changed after the due date under section
139(1).
However, Default is New tax regime. Central government trying to eliminate the Old tax regime
gradually. But in best interest of the taxpayers, we must provide the tax regime selection.
5.3 Pre-filling from AIS
The product must fetch the taxpayer's Annual Information Statement (AIS) at the start of every filing
session. AIS serves as the baseline reference for salary income, interest income, dividend income,
TDS credits, TCS credits, and advance tax payments. All data extracted from documents such as Form
16 must be reconciled against AIS. Where a difference is found, the discrepancy resolution process in
Section 3 applies. The product must not present unreconciled data to the taxpayer as confirmed
figures.
5.4 Document Capture and Data Extraction
The product must be capable of extracting income and deduction data from documents uploaded by the
taxpayer — including Form 16 (both Part A and Part B), bank interest certificates, home loan interest
certificates, premium receipts, and broker capital gains statements. Where documents are scanned
images rather than digital PDFs, the product must still be able to read and extract the relevant figures.
Any field where the extracted value cannot be confirmed with sufficient accuracy must be flagged for
the taxpayer to verify manually.
5.5 Deduction Interdependencies
Several deductions under Chapter VI-A have interdependencies that the product must enforce
automatically. The combined deduction under sections 80C, 80CCC, and 80CCD(1) cannot exceed
₹1,50,000. The deduction under 80CCD(1B) is a separate limit of ₹50,000 and is in addition to the
above. Sections 80EE and 80EEA cannot both be claimed in the same return. Sections 80TTA and
80TTB are mutually exclusive — only one applies depending on the taxpayer's age. The HRA
exemption under section 10(13A) and the deduction under section 80GG for rent paid cannot both be
claimed for the same period. The product must check these interdependencies after each deduction is
entered and alert the taxpayer immediately if a conflict is created.
