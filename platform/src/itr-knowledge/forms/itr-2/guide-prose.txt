5. Key Product Behaviour Requirements
5.1 ITR-2 Eligibility Check
The product must confirm ITR-2 eligibility before proceeding. If the taxpayer has any income from
business or profession — including freelance income, partnership income, or proprietary business —
ITR-2 is not applicable and the taxpayer must be directed to ITR-3 or ITR-4.
5.2 23 July 2024 Capital Gains Split
Every LTCG transaction must be automatically bucketed by transfer date. Transfers before 23 July
2024 are taxed at 20% with indexation. Transfers on or after 23 July 2024 are taxed at 12.5% without
indexation. The product must extract the transfer date from the uploaded document and apply the
correct rate without requiring the taxpayer to select it. The taxpayer may override with a stated reason.
5.3 Brought Forward Losses
Schedule CFL data must be auto-fetched from the portal's pre-fill. The taxpayer must be able to review
and override any pre-filled figure, with a mandatory reason field. The confirmed figures feed Schedule
BFLA and ultimately Part B-TI.
5.4 HUF-Specific Restrictions
Where the filing status is HUF, the product must automatically block deductions under 80CCD(1),
80CCD(1B), 80CCD(2), 80E, 80EE, 80EEA, 80EEB, 80U, and relief u/s 89. Schedule ESOP must also
be locked for HUF filers. These restrictions are applied at the field level and not left to the taxpayer to
identify.
5.5 VDA / Crypto Filing
A third-party tool integration (similar to KoinX) is required to handle crypto and VDA transaction
aggregation. The product must import the structured output of this tool into Schedule VDA and cross-
verify the total against the AIS VDA tile. This is to be scoped separately — see the footnote.
5.6 Section 80QQB and 80RRB — Filing Date Check
The product must check the filing date at the time of submission. If the return is being filed after the
section 139(1) due date, deductions under 80QQB and 80RRB must be automatically set to zero and
the taxpayer must be informed of this statutory disallowance before submission.
5.7 Non-Resident Restrictions
Where residential status is NRI, the product must block Schedule FSI, Schedule TR, rebate u/s 87A,
80DDB, 80DD, 80U, 80TTB, 80QQB, and 80RRB. It must also block Schedule FA filing requirements
prompts (as NRIs are exempt). These restrictions are applied automatically based on the residential
status selected.

Footnote — Items Requiring Separate Discussion
Need to discuss
1. Schedule VDA (Crypto / Virtual Digital Assets) — A third-party transaction aggregation tool (similar
to KoinX) must be integrated or built. The volume, multi-exchange nature, and cost-basis complexity of
VDA transactions make manual entry impractical. The precise integration architecture, data format, and
liability for calculation accuracy must be defined.
2. Schedule FSI (Foreign Income) — The data collection process for foreign tax paid, foreign income
by head, and country-wise TIN details involves documents that are not standardised in India. The flow
and the document types the product will accept must be scoped separately.
3. Schedule TR (DTAA Relief) — Form 67 filing is a prerequisite. Whether the product will guide the
taxpayer through Form 67 filing on the e-filing portal, or only verify that it has been filed, needs to be
decided.
4. Schedule 5A (Portuguese Civil Code) — Applicable to a small subset of taxpayers in Goa, Daman &
Diu. The product must determine whether this is handled as a self-declaration flow or requires a
separate review process
