# Old vs new tax regime, FY 2026-27

The old regime allows a wider range of deductions and exemptions; the new regime has lower slab rates and fewer deductions. The new regime is the default. The comparison assumes an individual below 60 with income at normal slab rates.

## Slab rates

New regime:

| Taxable income | Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹4,00,000 | Nil |
| ₹4,00,001 to ₹8,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹8,00,001 to ₹12,00,000 | 10% |
| ₹12,00,001 to ₹16,00,000 | 15% |
| ₹16,00,001 to ₹20,00,000 | 20% |
| ₹20,00,001 to ₹24,00,000 | 25% |
| Above ₹24,00,000 | 30% |

Old regime (below 60):

| Taxable income | Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to ₹2,50,000 | Nil |
| ₹2,50,001 to ₹5,00,000 | 5% |
| ₹5,00,001 to ₹10,00,000 | 20% |
| Above ₹10,00,000 | 30% |

## Key deductions and exemptions

| Deduction or exemption | Old regime | New regime |
|---|---|---|
| Standard deduction for salary or pension | ₹50,000 | ₹75,000 |
| Section 80C (eligible investments and expenses) | Up to ₹1,50,000 | Not available |
| Section 80D (medical insurance) | Available, subject to limits | Generally not available |
| HRA exemption | Available, subject to conditions | Not available |
| LTA exemption | Available, subject to conditions | Not available |
| Self-occupied home loan interest (section 24(b)) | Up to ₹2,00,000, subject to conditions | Not available |
| Employer's NPS contribution (section 80CCD(2)) | Available, subject to limits | Available, subject to limits |

## Rebate under section 87A

| | Old regime | New regime |
|---|---|---|
| Who | Resident individual | Resident individual |
| Total income up to | ₹5,00,000 | ₹12,00,000 |
| Maximum rebate | ₹12,500 | ₹60,000 |

With the ₹75,000 standard deduction, a resident salaried individual with salary up to ₹12,75,000 may pay nil tax under the new regime, if there is no special-rate income and the 87A rebate applies. Marginal relief may apply where total income is slightly above ₹12,00,000.

## What MyCA needs for a personal comparison

Annual figures for: gross salary and taxable allowances; HRA received and rent paid (and the city); 80C investments and eligible expenses; 80D premiums; home loan interest and property details; employer NPS contribution; other income (interest, capital gains, rent). `tax_plan` computes both regimes from these.

Section numbers above follow the Income-tax Act, 1961. The Income-tax Act, 2025 governs tax year 2026-27 onward and renumbers sections; MyCA's dated rules hold the figures for each year.
