The Adapter Invariant
External connectors are strict protocol translators. Remote payloads (from Quicko, GSTN, Tally, or Banks) are ingested and converted into existing MyCA schemas. Under no circumstances does an external API connector replace or duplicate internal accounting or reconciliation logic. All statutory uploads, OTPs, and signatures require authenticated CA approvals.
