Do and don't

Do

  • "Connects to LHDN MyInvois"
  • Exempt below RM3 million from 1 Sep 2026
  • Ink primary buttons; blue for links and structure
  • One gold bar or underline per layout
  • Fictional sample data, cited rules with dates

Don't

  • "LHDN-approved", "certified", "official"
  • Government logos, the flag or Jata Negara
  • Gold text on white, or gold buttons
  • Bahi worried in a headline, or holding money
  • Unreviewed machine-translated Malay
