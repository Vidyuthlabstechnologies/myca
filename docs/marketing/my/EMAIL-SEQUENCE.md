# MyCA Malaysia: launch email sequence (English and Bahasa Melayu)

For people who joined the waitlist or started a trial and **opted in** to emails (PDPA s43:
every email has an unsubscribe link; no purchased lists). Send in the language the person chose;
default English with a "Baca dalam Bahasa Melayu" link. From: "Bahi at MyCA". Plain layout, one
button, blue links, ink button.

| # | When | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Launch day (waitlist) / sign-up (trial) | Welcome, one action: install and check the threshold |
| 2 | Day 2 | Connect MyInvois (the intermediary step) |
| 3 | Day 4 | First e-invoice, approve-before-send |
| 4 | Day 7 | Consolidated e-invoice and the RM10,000 rule |
| 5 | Day 10 | SST-02 and inviting the accountant |
| 6 | Day 13 (trial end minus 1, if a trial is offered) | Choose a plan; what happens if you don't |

## Email 2: Connect MyInvois

**EN subject:** Ten minutes to connect MyInvois
**EN preview:** Add MyCA as your intermediary, with pictures.

Hi {first_name},

To send e-invoices for you, MyCA needs your permission in the MyInvois portal. You give it once,
as the company's director or authorised person:

1. Sign in to MyTax and open the MyInvois portal.
2. Go to your taxpayer profile, Representatives, Intermediary.
3. Add MyCA with the TIN and BRN shown in the app, set the dates, switch on the permissions.

The checklist in MyCA shows every screen, then tests the connection.

[Start the checklist]

**MS subjek:** Sepuluh minit untuk menyambung MyInvois
**MS pratonton:** Tambah MyCA sebagai perantara anda, bergambar.

Anda memberikannya sekali sahaja, sebagai pengarah syarikat atau orang yang diberi kuasa:

1. Log masuk ke MyTax dan buka portal MyInvois.
2. Pergi ke profil pembayar cukai, Wakil, Perantara.
3. Tambah MyCA dengan TIN dan BRN yang dipaparkan dalam aplikasi, tetapkan tarikh, hidupkan

## Email 3: First e-invoice

**EN subject:** Your first e-invoice, start to validated
**EN preview:** You'll see exactly what goes to LHDN.

Hi {first_name},

Try it with your next sale. Type it as you'd say it, for example "Sold 20 cartons to Syarikat
ABC, RM4,800", or fill the form. MyCA checks the buyer's TIN with LHDN and fills in the codes.
Then you see the whole e-invoice and tap Approve. LHDN's validation and QR link come back in
seconds.

Made a mistake? You can cancel within 72 hours of validation; after that, MyCA helps you issue a
credit note.

[Create an e-invoice]

**MS pratonton:** Anda akan melihat dengan tepat apa yang dihantar ke LHDN.

## Email 4: Consolidated e-invoice

**EN subject:** The monthly e-invoice for walk-in customers
**EN preview:** Due 7 days after month end. Sales above RM10,000 stay out.

Hi {first_name},

Customers who don't ask for an e-invoice still get a normal receipt, and their sales go into one
consolidated e-invoice each month, due within 7 calendar days after the month ends. Since
1 January 2026, any single sale above RM10,000 must have its own e-invoice. MyCA sorts this for
you and reminds you before the deadline.

[See this month's batch]

**MS subjek:** E-Invois bulanan untuk pelanggan biasa
**MS pratonton:** Perlu dihantar 7 hari selepas akhir bulan. Jualan melebihi RM10,000 diasingkan.

Hai {first_name},

Pelanggan yang tidak meminta e-Invois masih menerima resit biasa, dan jualan mereka dimasukkan ke
dalam satu e-Invois disatukan setiap bulan, yang perlu dihantar dalam tempoh 7 hari kalendar
selepas bulan berakhir. Sejak 1 Januari 2026, sebarang jualan tunggal melebihi RM10,000 mesti
mempunyai e-Invois sendiri. MyCA menguruskannya untuk anda dan mengingatkan anda sebelum tarikh
akhir.

[Lihat kelompok bulan ini]

## Email 5: SST and your accountant

**EN subject:** SST-02 without the spreadsheet
**EN preview:** And invite your accountant to the same books.

Hi {first_name},

If you're SST-registered, MyCA works out your SST-02 figures for each two-month period from your
books. You submit and pay in MySST by the last day of the following month.

Working with an accountant? Invite them in MyCA with their own role. They see the same books, and
you still approve what goes to LHDN.

[Invite my accountant]

**MS subjek:** SST-02 tanpa hamparan
**MS pratonton:** Dan jemput akauntan anda ke buku akaun yang sama.

Hai {first_name},

Jika anda berdaftar SST, MyCA mengira angka SST-02 anda bagi setiap tempoh dua bulan daripada buku
akaun anda. Anda menghantar dan membayar di MySST sebelum hari terakhir bulan berikutnya.

Bekerja dengan akauntan? Jemput mereka dalam MyCA dengan peranan sendiri. Mereka melihat buku
akaun yang sama, dan anda masih meluluskan apa yang dihantar ke LHDN.

[Jemput akauntan saya]
