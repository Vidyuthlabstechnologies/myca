# MyCA Malaysia marketing plan

Version 1.0, 14 November 2026. For the owner. Every fact about the market or a competitor has a
source with the date it was checked; competitor prices change often, so re-check before any
comparison is published.

## 1. The moment

- **E-invoicing is mandatory for businesses with annual turnover of RM3 million and above**; below
  RM3 million is exempt from 1 September 2026 (raised from RM1 million; LHDN says more than
  1.1 million businesses benefit). Source: The Star, 30 August 2026,
  https://thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/08/30/over-11-million-businesses-to-benefit-from-higher-e-invoicing-threshold-says-lhdn
  and IRBM e-Invoice Guideline v4.8 (30 August 2026), https://www.hasil.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/IRBM-e-Invoice-Guideline.pdf
- **Phase 4 (RM3m to RM5m) has been in interim relaxation**, consolidating everything except
  sales above RM10,000; relaxation was extended to 31 December 2026 (Grant Thornton, 6 January
  2026, https://www.grantthornton.com.my/insights/Tax/Tax-Alert-6-January-2026/). One secondary
  source reports a further extension to end-2027; until LHDN confirms, messaging says "when the
  relaxation ends" and never names a date as a threat.
- Owners are confused and tired of changing rules: only 30% of SMEs fully understand
  e-invoicing; 70% want step-by-step guides or checklists; 55% cite unclear rules; 47% data
  security (Xero whitepaper, 500 respondents, via Malaysia SME,
  https://www.malaysiasme.com.my/malaysias-smes-need-more-support-ahead-of-e-invoicing-mandate-xero-whitepaper-finds/).
  Frustration with "flip-flops" (The Rakyat Post, 8 December 2025,
  https://www.therakyatpost.com/news/2025/12/08/malaysians-are-upset-over-e-invoicing-flip-flops-by-the-government/).

The opening for MyCA: **the calm, phone-first way to get e-invoicing and SST right, with a
checklist, a helper that cites the rule, and nothing sent without your approval.**

What this means:

- **MyCA is 2 to 10 times the price** of the cloud packages SMEs compare against, and LHDN's
  portal is free. MyCA cannot win on price or on accounting breadth against SQL and AutoCount,
  which have dealer networks and years of local features.
- MyCA can win on: phone-first, bilingual, **approve-before-send** safety, a checklist that does
  the MyInvois setup with the owner, the consolidated-batch rules built in, Bahi answering with
  the rule cited, and the owner and accountant on the same books.
- For **ICP B (firms)**, RM1,999 a month for many clients can be cheaper than per-client
  subscriptions on other packages; this is MyCA's strongest price story.

## 4. Positioning

**For** Malaysian business owners who must e-invoice and want it right without learning MyInvois,
**MyCA Malaysia** is the accountant in your pocket that prepares every e-invoice, SST figure and
tax estimate and **sends nothing to LHDN until you approve it**. **Unlike** desktop packages and
the bare MyInvois portal, MyCA works from your phone in English or Malay, walks you through setup
step by step, and tells you which rule applies and why.

Tagline options:
- EN: "E-invoices, SST and books. You approve each one."
- MS: "E-Invois, SST dan buku akaun. Anda yang meluluskan."
- EN: "Right first time with LHDN."
- MS: "Betul pada kali pertama dengan LHDN."

Proof points to build before paid spend: 10 pilot businesses with quotes (permission in writing),
time to first validated e-invoice (target under 15 minutes after the portal step), number of
LHDN rejections avoided by the TIN check.

## 6. Pricing recommendation (owner decides)

Keep RM299 / RM699 / RM1,999 only if each plan includes a visible **"done-with-you"** service:
a 30-minute onboarding video call, the MyInvois intermediary step done together, and WhatsApp
support in English and Malay within business hours. Otherwise expect low conversion against
RM35 to RM180 alternatives. Options:

1. Add a **Starter** tier around RM79 to RM99 a month (e-invoices and consolidated batch only).
2. Offer **annual** base plans with two months free.
3. Launch offer: first month free on SME (Play free trial), no card games.

## 8. Measurement

North-star: **businesses with a validated e-invoice in the last 30 days**. Funnel: install,
account, MyInvois connected, first validated e-invoice, paid. Targets for the first 90 days to be
set after pilots; track by channel with UTM parameters and Play's acquisition reports.

## 9. Claims and compliance checklist for every asset

- [ ] No "LHDN-approved/certified/official", no government logos or flag.
- [ ] Exemption threshold stated as RM3 million (from 1 September 2026).
- [ ] Income tax and SST described as prepared by MyCA and filed by the user.
- [ ] Prices match Play Console; "billed through Google Play, cancel any time".
- [ ] Competitor comparisons only with dated, sourced facts, and fair (PDPA and the Consumer
