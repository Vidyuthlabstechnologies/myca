# MyCA Malaysia: 10 launch social posts (English and Bahasa Melayu)

Each post has an EN and an MS version (publish as separate posts per audience, not mixed in one
caption). Visual templates: `assets/social-*.html`, rendered in `assets/png/`. Square 1080×1080
for Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn; reuse the headline on a 1080×1920 story frame.

Rules: no government logos or flag; exemption = below RM3 million from 1 September 2026; no fear
claims about penalties; every rule post names the source in the caption. Hashtags: at most three.

### 2. Threshold check

**Visual:** `social-02-threshold` · "Below RM3 million? You don't have to e-invoice yet."

EN: Good news for smaller businesses: from 1 September 2026, LHDN exempts businesses with annual
turnover below RM3 million from e-invoicing (unless a related company is RM3 million or more).
Not sure where you stand? MyCA checks in four questions and shows you the rule. Source: IRBM
e-Invoice Guideline v4.8.

MS: Berita baik untuk perniagaan kecil: mulai 1 September 2026, LHDN mengecualikan perniagaan
dengan jualan tahunan di bawah RM3 juta daripada e-Invois (kecuali syarikat berkaitan mencapai
RM3 juta atau lebih). Tidak pasti? MyCA menyemak dalam empat soalan dan menunjukkan peraturannya.
Sumber: Garis Panduan e-Invois LHDN v4.8.

### 3. The RM10,000 rule

**Visual:** `social-03-rm10k` · "Over RM10,000? It needs its own e-invoice."

EN: Since 1 January 2026, any single sale above RM10,000 cannot go into a consolidated e-invoice,
in every industry. MyCA spots these sales and keeps them out of your monthly batch automatically.
Source: IRBM e-Invoice Specific Guideline v4.9, Table 3.6.

MS: Sejak 1 Januari 2026, sebarang jualan tunggal melebihi RM10,000 tidak boleh dimasukkan ke
dalam e-Invois disatukan, bagi semua industri. MyCA mengesan jualan ini dan mengasingkannya
daripada kelompok bulanan anda secara automatik. Sumber: Garis Panduan Khusus e-Invois LHDN v4.9,
Jadual 3.6.

### 4. Seven days

**Visual:** `social-04-7days` · calendar with day 7 circled in gold

EN: Consolidated e-invoice for last month? It's due within 7 calendar days after the month ends.
MyCA builds it from your sales and reminds you before the deadline. You approve, we submit.

MS: E-Invois disatukan untuk bulan lepas? Ia perlu dihantar dalam tempoh 7 hari kalendar selepas
bulan berakhir. MyCA menyediakannya daripada jualan anda dan mengingatkan anda sebelum tarikh
akhir. Anda luluskan, kami hantar.

### 5. Approve-before-send

**Visual:** `social-05-approve` · phone with the Approve screen

### 6. 72 hours

**Visual:** carousel, 3 slides

EN: Made a mistake on an e-invoice? You can cancel it within 72 hours of LHDN's validation. After
that, you fix it with a credit note or debit note. MyCA shows the right button at the right time.
Source: IRBM e-Invoice Guideline v4.8, s2.3.6.

MS: Tersilap pada e-Invois? Anda boleh membatalkannya dalam tempoh 72 jam selepas pengesahan LHDN.
Selepas itu, betulkan dengan nota kredit atau nota debit. MyCA menunjukkan butang yang betul pada
masa yang betul. Sumber: Garis Panduan e-Invois LHDN v4.8, s2.3.6.
