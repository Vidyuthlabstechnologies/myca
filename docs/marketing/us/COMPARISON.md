## How we talk about it

MyCA US is not the cheapest way to file, and the page says so. Two of these products file a federal
return for $0 for many or all people. The page's job is to explain what you get for the fee, not to
pretend the others are worse.

**Headline:** What you pay for with MyCA US

**Intro:**
> If you want the lowest possible price, free options exist, and some people should use them. MyCA
> US costs $129 to file ($99 through April 15, 2027) because it is built for people who want to
> understand their return: every line opens to show where it came from, you see your refund before
> you pay, and you're told up front if your return needs something we don't handle.

**Three honest differences:**

1. **One price for the federal return.** No tiers that change as you answer questions. Freelance
   income, investments and itemizing are included in the same $129.
2. **Explained, not just finished.** Each line on your Form 1040 shows the form, answer or rule
   behind it.
3. **Told before you pay.** If your return needs a form MyCA doesn't support yet, you'll know before
   you're charged.

**Where others may suit you better (say it):**

- You need a state return filed: MyCA doesn't file state returns this season.
- You want a professional to prepare or review your return now: Expert Review isn't open yet.
- You want to pay nothing: free options from some providers above cover many returns.
