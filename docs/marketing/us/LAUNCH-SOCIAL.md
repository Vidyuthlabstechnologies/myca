## TikTok and Reels script (30 to 35 seconds)

**Title on cover:** "Do you actually know why your refund is that number?"

|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Close-up of a phone, a paper W-2 next to it on a kitchen table | VO: "Quick question. Do you know why your refund is the number it is?" |
| 3–7 s | Hand snaps the W-2 with the MyCA camera | VO: "This is MyCA US. Snap your W-2..." Text: "Snap your W-2" |
| 7–12 s | Screen shows boxes read one by one, a finger taps "Looks right" | VO: "...it reads every box, and you check each one." |
| 12–19 s | Review screen; tap a line, sheet opens showing "From your W-2, box 1" | VO: "Then tap any line on your return and it shows you where it came from." Text: "Every line explained" |
| 19–24 s | Refund estimate screen, amount blurred, label "Sample return" | VO: "You see your refund before you pay anything." Text: "Free to prepare" |
| 24–29 s | Bahi wave animation on the canvas background | VO: "When it's right, you file. Ninety-nine dollars through April fifteenth." Text: "$99 to file through 4/15/2027" |
| 29–33 s | End card: wordmark, store badges | Text: "MyCA US. Federal returns only. Not affiliated with the IRS." |

Production notes: real hands, real kitchen light, no stock "money falling" footage. Screens come
from the sandbox build with the sample household; the refund amount is blurred or labelled "Sample
return". Captions burned in for sound-off viewing. Keep text out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px.
