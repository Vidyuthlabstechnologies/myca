# MyCA US FAQ

For the website, the in-app help and support macros. Answers are short and exact; anything we are
not sure of is not promised. Bracketed items are owner blanks.

## Getting started

**What is MyCA US?**
An app that prepares your federal income tax return (Form 1040) on your phone, explains every line,
and gets it e-filed through our IRS-authorized e-file partner.

**When can I use it?**
MyCA US opens in January 2027 for your 2026 return. You can prepare your return as soon as the app is
live; e-filing opens when the IRS opens the filing season, usually in late January.

**Which phones does it work on?**
Android phones from the Google Play Store [and iPhone from the App Store, once that build is live].

**Is MyCA US connected to the IRS?**
No. MyCA is a private company's app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the IRS or any
government agency. Your return is e-filed through an IRS-authorized e-file provider that we partner
with.

## Price

**Is it free?**
Preparing your return, checking it, seeing your refund estimate and downloading a PDF are free. You
pay only when you file: $129, or $99 through April 15, 2027.

**When am I charged?**
Only when you choose to file, after you have seen your full return. Your card is charged once for
that tax year's federal return.

**What is MyCA Plus?**
An optional plan for $249 a year. It works out your quarterly estimated tax payments, checks
whether your paycheck withholding is on track, and helps you read and answer IRS letters. It renews
each year until you cancel, and is billed through Google Play [or the App Store].

**Can a tax professional review my return?**
Expert Review is coming soon. Join the waitlist in the app and we'll let you know when it opens. You
can already share your return with your own tax professional from the app.

**Do you charge to file a state return?**
We don't file state returns this season. MyCA gives you the federal numbers your state's own filing
site asks for.

## What it covers

**Which income and forms does MyCA handle?**
Wages (W-2), interest (1099-INT), dividends (1099-DIV), investment sales (1099-B, Form 8949 and
Schedule D), freelance and gig income (1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, Schedule C and Schedule SE),
unemployment (1099-G), retirement distributions (1099-R), Social Security (SSA-1099), mortgage
interest (1098), student loan interest (1098-E) and tuition (1098-T, Form 8863). It also handles the
Child Tax Credit (Form 8812), the Earned Income Tax Credit, the qualified business income deduction
(Form 8995), and the standard or itemized deduction (Schedule A).

**What doesn't it handle yet?**
Some situations, for example rental income (Schedule E). If your return needs a form MyCA doesn't
handle, the app tells you before you pay, so you can file another way.

**Can I file a joint return?**
Yes. Married filing jointly is supported, and you can invite your spouse to see the return.

**Can I file for a past year or amend a return?**
Not this season. MyCA prepares 2026 returns.

## Accuracy and the IRS

**How do I know the numbers are right?**
The maths is done by a tax engine with fixed rules, not by a chatbot. Every line shows the form,
answer or rule it came from, and MyCA runs checks before you sign. You still review the return and
are responsible for what you file.

**What is your accuracy promise?**
If a mistake in MyCA's calculations means your federal return needs to be corrected, we fix it and
you file the correction at no extra charge. This promise does not cover IRS penalties or interest.

**What if the IRS rejects my return?**
MyCA shows the IRS's reason in plain words and takes you to the step that fixes it. Fix it and file
again; there's no second charge for refiling the same return.

**How long until I get my refund?**
The IRS decides that. It says most refunds for e-filed returns with direct deposit are issued in
less than 21 days, and some take longer. MyCA does not offer refund advances or loans.

**Does MyCA guarantee my refund amount?**
No. Your refund is set by your tax situation and the IRS. MyCA shows its estimate and how it got
there.

## Privacy and security

**What happens to my information?**
It is used only to prepare and file your return, as federal law (IRC section 7216) requires. We
never sell it or use it for ads.

**Where is it stored?**
Encrypted, on servers in the United States. Social Security numbers are sealed with a separate key
and shown only as the last four digits.

**Does the AI assistant see my Social Security number?**
No. Social Security numbers are removed before anything reaches the assistant or the form reader.

**Can I delete my account?**
Yes, in the app. Records the law requires a tax preparer to keep (such as the signed Form 8879) are
kept for the required period and then deleted.
