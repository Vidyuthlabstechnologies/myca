**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

## MyCA US launches a phone-first federal tax app that explains every line of your return

*Free to prepare and see your refund. $99 to file through April 15, 2027.*

**[CITY, State], [Month Day], 2027** — [US company name] today launched MyCA US, an app for iPhone
and Android that prepares a federal income tax return (Form 1040) on your phone and shows, line by
line, where every number came from.

Most people finish their taxes without knowing why the refund is what it is. MyCA US is built
around the opposite idea: every line on the return can be tapped to see the form, the answer, or
the rule that produced it. People add their W-2s, 1099s and 1098s by photo, PDF or typing; MyCA
reads each form box by box and asks the person to confirm the amounts before they are saved.

"Doing your taxes shouldn't feel like signing something you can't read," said [Name], [title] of
[US company name]. "MyCA shows you your refund before you pay anything, explains how it got there,
and tells you up front if your return needs something we don't handle yet."

**What MyCA US does**

- Prepares Form 1040 with the schedules most households need, including Schedule C and Schedule SE
  for freelance and gig income, Schedule A for itemized deductions, Schedule D and Form 8949 for
  investment sales, and the Child Tax Credit, Earned Income Tax Credit and education credits.
- Reads W-2, 1099 and 1098 forms from a photo or PDF, and asks the filer to check each box.
- Shows the refund or amount owed as the return is built, free, before any payment.
- Hands the finished return to an IRS-authorized e-file partner, prefilled, for signing and
  filing, and shows when the IRS accepts it. If the IRS rejects a return, MyCA explains why and
  links to the fix.
- Keeps returns encrypted on US servers and never sells tax information or uses it for ads.

**Pricing**

Preparing a return and seeing the refund are free. Filing a federal return costs $129, with a
launch price of $99 through April 15, 2027. MyCA Plus, $249 a year, adds quarterly estimated-payment
planning, a withholding check and help reading IRS letters. Expert Review by a credentialed tax
professional is coming later; people can join the waitlist in the app.

**Accuracy promise**

If a mistake in MyCA's calculations means a federal return needs to be corrected, MyCA fixes it and
the filer files the correction at no extra charge. The promise does not cover IRS penalties or
interest.

**Availability**

MyCA US is available now on the App Store and Google Play in the United States. It prepares federal
returns for tax year 2026; state returns are not offered this season.

**About [US company name]**

[US company name] makes MyCA, a family of apps that keep personal and small-business finances in
order, with the maths done by deterministic tax engines and every figure explained. MyCA US is not
affiliated with the IRS or any government agency.
