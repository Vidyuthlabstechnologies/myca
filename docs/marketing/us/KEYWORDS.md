# MyCA US keyword list

November 14, 2026. For store search (ASO), the website and paid search. Volumes are not given
because we have no measured data; the owner should pull them from Apple Search Ads and Google Ads
Keyword Planner before setting bids. Intent and fit are our judgement.

## Rules

- Never bid on or use competitor trademarks as keywords in the stores (Apple rejects it) or in ad
  text. Bidding on competitor names in Google Ads is allowed by Google but invites trademark
  complaints; leave it out of the launch plan.
- Never target "free tax filing" alone: MyCA is free to prepare, not free to file. Use it only as a
  negative keyword or with ad copy that states the filing fee in the headline.
- Do not target words that suggest a government service ("IRS free file", "Direct File").
