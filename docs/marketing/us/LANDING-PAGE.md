## Three steps

1. **Add your forms.** Snap a W-2, 1099 or 1098, upload the PDF, or type it in. You check every box
   before it's saved.
2. **Answer a few questions.** One at a time, in plain English. Your refund updates as you go.
3. **Review, sign, file.** Every line shows where it came from. When it's right, you sign and
   e-file through our IRS-authorized partner.

## Accuracy promise

If a mistake in MyCA's calculations means your federal return needs to be corrected, we fix it and
you file the correction at no extra charge. This promise does not cover IRS penalties or interest.

## Who it's for

- **Paycheck people.** One W-2 or three, plus bank interest. Ten to twenty minutes with your forms
  in hand.
- **Freelancers and gig workers.** 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC income and your business expenses on
  Schedule C, with self-employment tax worked out.
- **Young families.** Dependents, the Child Tax Credit, child care and head of household, worked out
  from simple questions.
- **Retirees.** Social Security, pensions and IRA distributions, with what's taxable explained.

## FAQ (short; full list in FAQ.md)

- When can I file? — When the IRS opens the 2027 season, usually late January. You can start
  preparing as soon as the app is live.
- Does it do state returns? — Not this season. MyCA gives you the federal numbers your state's
  filing site asks for.
- Is it really free to prepare? — Yes. You see your full return and refund estimate free. You pay
  only when you file.
- Who files my return? — Our IRS-authorized e-file partner, with your details prefilled. You sign
  and file in the app's filing step.
