# MyCA US marketing

Brand and launch material for MyCA US, the separate US app (`com.mldynamic.myca.us`) for the 2027
filing season. Start with the brand kit; everything else follows it.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [BRAND-KIT.md](BRAND-KIT.md) | Name, logo, color with contrast pairs, type, Bahi, voice, US English, legal lines |
| [STORE-LISTING.md](STORE-LISTING.md) | Google Play and App Store copy, keywords field, screenshot captions |
| [KEYWORDS.md](KEYWORDS.md) | Keyword clusters for stores, site and paid search, with negatives |
| [LANDING-PAGE.md](LANDING-PAGE.md) | Copy for `/us` |
| [FAQ.md](FAQ.md) | Website, in-app help and support answers |
| [COMPARISON.md](COMPARISON.md) | Dated, cited facts on TurboTax, H&R Block, FreeTaxUSA, Cash App Taxes |
| [ONBOARDING-EMAILS.md](ONBOARDING-EMAILS.md) | Three-email sequence after sign-up |
| [LAUNCH-SOCIAL.md](LAUNCH-SOCIAL.md) | X, LinkedIn, Instagram posts and a TikTok/Reels script |

| [LAUNCH-PLAN-90-DAY.md](LAUNCH-PLAN-90-DAY.md) | January 18 to April 17, 2027 |
| [assets/](assets/) | Icons, wordmarks, Play feature graphic, social cards; `node docs/marketing/us/assets/build.mjs` rebuilds them |

## Blanks only the owner can fill

- US company legal name and postal address (footer, emails, press release, store copyright)
- Domain for the US site and the support, hello and press email addresses
- Refund policy for the filing fee, and the accuracy promise in the Terms of Service
- The e-file partner's consent to be described as "our IRS-authorized e-file partner"
- Paid budget and the target cost per filed return
- Social handles and the press contact
