# Onboarding email sequence (3 emails)

For people who create a MyCA US account. Sender: **MyCA US** `<hello@[domain]>`; reply-to a real,
read inbox `[support email]`.

## Rules for every email

- **No tax return information in email.** No refund amounts, income, form names from their return,
  dependents or filing status. Using return information to market to someone needs their separate
  written consent under IRC section 7216 and Treasury Regulation 301.7216-3. These emails are
  triggered only by account events (signed up, opened the app, not yet filed), never by what is in
  the return.
- Triggers that are safe: account created, days since sign-up, "has not opened the return in N
  days", "has filed" (to stop the sequence). The filing state is used only to stop sending.
- CAN-SPAM: the company's postal address, a working one-click unsubscribe, honest subject lines.
  These are relationship emails, but they carry promotional content (the launch price), so treat
  them as commercial.
- Plain text plus a light HTML version; no images needed except the logo. Under 150 words of body.
- Stop the sequence as soon as the person files or unsubscribes.

>
> Welcome to MyCA US. Your account is set up.
>
> Here's the quickest way to start:
>
> 1. Open the app and tap **Tax documents**.
> 2. Snap your W-2 (or any 1099 or 1098). MyCA reads it box by box, and you check each amount.
> 3. Answer a few questions, one at a time.
>
> Your refund estimate updates as you go. Preparing and checking your return is free; you pay only
> when you file ($99 through April 15, 2027).
>
> Don't have your forms yet? Employers must send W-2s by January 31. Start with the questions about
> you and your family, and add forms when they arrive.
>
> — The MyCA US team
>
> [Open MyCA US]

## Email 2 — day 3, only if no tax document has been added

**Subject:** Waiting on your W-2?
**Preview text:** What to gather now so filing takes one sitting.

> Hi [first name],
>
> Most people start their return when the first form arrives. Here's a short list so nothing
> surprises you later:
>
> - **W-2** from each job you had in 2026
> - **1099s**: 1099-NEC for freelance work, 1099-INT and 1099-DIV from banks and brokers, 1099-B for
>   investments you sold, 1099-R for retirement withdrawals, SSA-1099 for Social Security
> - **1098s**: mortgage interest, student loan interest (1098-E), tuition (1098-T)
> - Last year's adjusted gross income, which you'll need to sign your e-filed return
>
> When you add a form, MyCA shows you which line of your Form 1040 each box lands on.
>
> — The MyCA US team
>
> [Add a form]

## Email 3 — day 10, only if the person has not filed

**Subject:** What happens when you tap File
**Preview text:** Exactly what you pay, what you sign, and when the IRS answers.

> Hi [first name],
>
> No surprises at the end, so here's the last step in full:
>
> - **Review.** Every line of your return, with where it came from. Anything that needs fixing links
>   to the step that fixes it.
> - **Pay.** $99 through April 15, 2027 ($129 after). You see the price before you start, and
>   nothing is charged until you choose to file.
> - **Sign and file.** Your return goes to our IRS-authorized e-file partner with your details
>   filled in. You sign and send.
> - **Hear back.** MyCA shows when the IRS accepts it. If it's sent back, we tell you why and how to
>   fix it.
>
> Our accuracy promise: if a mistake in MyCA's calculations means your return needs correcting, we
> fix it and you file the correction at no extra charge. (It doesn't cover IRS penalties or
> interest.)
>
> — The MyCA US team
>
> [Finish my return]
