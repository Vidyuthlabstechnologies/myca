## FAQ

**Is MyCA UK recognised by HMRC?**
MyCA UK is built for Making Tax Digital and has been tested against HMRC's test service. Our
application for HMRC recognition is in progress. We will say so here as soon as HMRC lists us.

**Do I need Making Tax Digital for Income Tax?**
If you are a sole trader or landlord and your self-employment and property income together, before
expenses, was over £50,000, you have needed it since 6 April 2026. Over £30,000 joins on
6 April 2027 and over £20,000 on 6 April 2028. HMRC uses the return from the earlier year. Check
your own position on GOV.UK.

**When are the quarterly updates due?**
For the 2026-27 tax year the updates were due on 7 August and 7 November 2026, and the next are due
on 7 February and 7 May 2027 [check before publishing]. MyCA shows your own dates, read from HMRC.

**What happens if I am late?**
Each late quarterly update or final declaration earns a penalty point, and four points bring a £200
penalty. If you started in April 2026, HMRC is not giving points for late quarterly updates in your
first four quarters. People who start in 2027 or 2028 do not get that. MyCA reminds you well before
each deadline.

**Does MyCA do my tax return?**
MyCA prepares your quarterly updates and final declaration from your records and sends them when
you approve. You stay in control and responsible for what is sent. MyCA is software, not tax
advice; if your affairs are complex, talk to an accountant (who can use MyCA with you).

**What if MyCA gets something wrong?**
If a figure we sent was wrong because of our error, we fix it and help you refile for free. We do
not promise outcomes with HMRC or pay penalties.

**Do you use AI?**
Our assistant uses AI to understand your question and choose which MyCA tool to run. The figures
always come from MyCA's fixed rules, never from the AI. It cannot send anything to HMRC.

**Where is my data kept?**
On Google Cloud in London. We never sell it or use it for advertising. See our privacy notice.

**What does MyCA send to HMRC about my phone?**
HMRC asks all Making Tax Digital software to send some details about your device and connection
with each request, to help prevent fraud. We list exactly what on our fraud prevention data page.

**Can I bring my records from another app or a spreadsheet?**
Yes. Import bank statements and spreadsheets, and our team can help you move across.

**Can I cancel?**
Any time, in Google Play. Your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for, and you can export
everything.

**Is there a free plan?**
No. Every plan has a 14-day free trial so you can try it with your own records first.

**Does it work in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland?**
Yes. The tax estimate uses Scottish income tax rates for Scottish taxpayers.
