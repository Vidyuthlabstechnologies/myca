## Where the market is (November 2026)

- **MTD for Income Tax is live.** Since 6 April 2026, sole traders and landlords with qualifying
  income (self-employment plus property, before expenses) over **£50,000** must keep digital
  records and send quarterly updates through software. The first two updates of 2026-27 were due
  7 August and 7 November 2026. The next are due **7 February 2027** and **7 May 2027** (check
  before publishing: gb.md has not confirmed these two on GOV.UK).
- **The second wave** joins on **6 April 2027**: qualifying income over **£30,000**, read from the
  2025-26 return, which is due **31 January 2027**. These people are filing now and deciding on
  software over the winter.
- **The third wave** joins on **6 April 2028**: over **£20,000**.
- **Penalty points**: one point per late quarterly update or final declaration; four points bring a
  £200 penalty. April 2026 starters get no points for late quarterly updates in their first four
  quarters; 2027 and 2028 starters do not get that easement (gb.md).
- From September 2026 HMRC has been signing up people who should have joined and have not.
- **MTD for VAT** already covers every VAT-registered business. The VAT registration threshold is
  £90,000 (gb.md; confirm on GOV.UK).

## What we promise

1. **Nothing goes to HMRC until you approve it.** Every return and update has a review screen.
2. **Figures you can check.** Every number comes from fixed rules and shows where it came from; the
   assistant only picks tools and never invents a figure. HMRC's own calculation is shown beside ours.
3. **Free fix-and-refile if our error.** No penalty guarantees; a plain promise to put our mistakes
   right at no charge.
4. **Your data stays in the UK.** Stored on Google Cloud in London.
5. **The whole year in one app.** Digital records, quarterly updates for self-employment and
   property, VAT, the tax estimate (including Scottish rates, Class 4 National Insurance, student
   loans, the High Income Child Benefit Charge, dividends, savings, capital gains and payments on
   account), the final declaration and every deadline.
6. **Built for Making Tax Digital, HMRC recognition in progress.** Switch to "HMRC recognised" only
   after HMRC lists MyCA.

### Premium service, concretely

We charge more (£49 a month for a sole trader), so the service must feel it:

- **Done-for-you set-up**: we bring across last year's figures and the records so far this tax year
  from a spreadsheet or another app, and check them with the customer on a call. [Owner to confirm

  quarterly deadline, extended hours.
- **Deadline concierge**: reminders 14, 7 and 1 days before each deadline, and a nudge if the

- **Quarter health check**: before each update, a plain list of what looks odd.
- **Free fix-and-refile if our error.**
- **No upsell walls**: one price, every feature for that plan.
