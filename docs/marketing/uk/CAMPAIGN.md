# MyCA UK launch campaign

Draft 2026-11-14. Built around the MTD for Income Tax waves in gb.md. Every date marked
**[check]** must be confirmed on GOV.UK before it is published. No public message may say MyCA can
file with HMRC for real until HMRC has granted production access; until then the line is
"built for Making Tax Digital, HMRC recognition in progress" and calls to action are "join the
waiting list" or "start your trial" (books, estimates and reminders work without HMRC production
access; submissions do not).

## Phases

### Phase 0: before HMRC production access (now until approval)

Goal: a waiting list of 5,000 UK sole traders and landlords, and 50 accountant firms. [Targets to
confirm.]

- Landing page live with the waiting list and the 14-day trial for records, estimates and reminders.
- SEO content (list below) published, one or two pages a week.
- LinkedIn founder posts and accountant outreach.
- No paid acquisition beyond small tests.

### Phase 1: "Two quarters in" (from HMRC approval, aimed at January to February 2027)

Audience: 2026 starters who are unhappy with their first choice, and accountants with MTD clients.
Message: "Two quarters in. Now make it calm." Move across before the 7 February update, with
help bringing records over.

- Email the waiting list the day production access is granted.
- Paid search on switching and quarterly update terms.
- Accountant webinars: "Running the February quarter for 50 clients".

### Phase 2: "Ready by April" (February to April 2027)

Audience: 2027 starters, who will have just filed their 2025-26 return.
Message: "Making Tax Digital starts for you on 6 April 2027. Be ready by February." (Then "by
March", then "this week".)

- Content on "Do I need MTD?", the qualifying income rule, setting up for the first quarter.
- Paid social to self-employed trades, consultants, landlords.
- Partnerships: landlord associations, trade bodies, coworking spaces [to approach].

### Phase 3: "No more grace" (May to August 2027)

Audience: both waves. Message: the first-year easement for 2026 starters ends after four quarters;
from the 7 August 2027 update, late updates earn points for everyone. Calm, factual reminders.

### Phase 4: 2028 wave nurture (autumn 2027 onwards)

Audience: over £20,000. Low-cost content and email nurture; trial pushes from January 2028.
