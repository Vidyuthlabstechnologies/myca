# MyCA Malaysia: Play Store listing (English and Bahasa Melayu)

Drafted 14 November 2026 for `com.mldynamic.myca.my`. The owner and a native Malay reviewer
approve the wording before anything is submitted. Limits (Play Console, "Add product details"):
app name 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4,000, for full- and half-width
characters alike. Character counts below were checked with a script on 2026-11-14.

Rules followed: no "LHDN-approved" or "certified"; no government logos; we do not say MyCA files
income tax or SST (the user does, in MyTax and MySST); the e-invoice exemption is stated as
below RM3 million (from 1 September 2026); no prices in the title; no emoji; no repeated keywords.

Source for limits: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152
(accessed 2026-11-14).

### Full description (4,000 max)

```listing-full
MyCA Malaysia keeps your business books the way a careful accountant would, from your phone, in English or Bahasa Melayu.

E-invoices to LHDN MyInvois, with your approval
Add MyCA as your intermediary in the MyInvois portal once, with our step-by-step checklist. Then create an e-invoice in a few taps. MyCA checks the buyer's TIN, fills in the codes LHDN asks for, signs the document and submits it to MyInvois only after you tap Approve. You see LHDN's validation result and QR link straight away.

Consolidated e-invoices without the guesswork
For sales to customers who don't ask for an e-invoice, MyCA builds the monthly consolidated e-invoice for you and reminds you before the 7-day deadline. Sales above RM10,000 are kept out of the batch, as the rules require.

Do I have to e-invoice?
Answer a few questions and MyCA tells you, with the rule it used. Businesses below RM3 million in annual turnover are exempt from 1 September 2026 and can still start voluntarily.

SST made clear
MyCA works out your sales tax and service tax from your books for each two-month period and lays out the SST-02 figures, ready for you to submit and pay in MySST.

Income tax estimate
Get your Form BE or Form B figures ready, with the reliefs you can claim. You file in MyTax yourself; MyCA shows you what to enter.

Everything else a small business needs
- Invoices, quotations, credit notes and debit notes
- Record sales, bills and expenses by typing a sentence, or photograph a bill
- Bank lines matched to your books
- Profit and loss, balance sheet, who owes you and who you owe
- Invite your accountant or staff, each with their own role
- Export everything at any time, free

Ask Bahi
Bahi, MyCA's helper, answers questions about e-invoicing and SST with the source, in plain words.

Plans (billed monthly through Google Play)
- SME: RM299 a month
- Growth: RM699 a month
- Firm: RM1,999 a month, for accounting firms looking after many clients
Subscriptions renew until you cancel in Google Play.

Your data
Your books are encrypted and stored on Google Cloud in Singapore, sent only over HTTPS. Nothing goes to LHDN or anyone else until you approve it. You can download or delete your data from the app.

MyCA is not affiliated with, endorsed by or part of LHDN, the Royal Malaysian Customs Department or the Government of Malaysia, and is not a tax agent. Official sources: MyTax https://mytax.hasil.gov.my, MyInvois https://myinvois.hasil.gov.my, MySST https://mysst.customs.gov.my.

MyCA is made by ML Dynamic Solutions Private Limited.
```

```listing-full-ms
MyCA Malaysia menyimpan buku akaun perniagaan anda seperti akauntan yang teliti, terus dari telefon anda, dalam Bahasa Melayu atau Bahasa Inggeris.

E-Invois ke LHDN MyInvois, dengan kelulusan anda
Tambah MyCA sebagai perantara anda di portal MyInvois sekali sahaja, dengan senarai semak langkah demi langkah kami. Kemudian buat e-Invois dengan beberapa sentuhan. MyCA menyemak TIN pembeli, mengisi kod yang diminta oleh LHDN, menandatangani dokumen dan menghantarnya ke MyInvois hanya selepas anda menekan Luluskan. Anda melihat keputusan pengesahan LHDN dan pautan QR serta-merta.

E-Invois disatukan tanpa teka-teki
Untuk jualan kepada pelanggan yang tidak meminta e-Invois, MyCA menyediakan e-Invois disatukan bulanan untuk anda dan mengingatkan anda sebelum tarikh akhir 7 hari. Jualan melebihi RM10,000 diasingkan, seperti yang dikehendaki peraturan.

Adakah saya perlu mengeluarkan e-Invois?
Jawab beberapa soalan dan MyCA akan memberitahu anda, berserta peraturan yang digunakan. Perniagaan dengan jualan tahunan di bawah RM3 juta dikecualikan mulai 1 September 2026 dan masih boleh bermula secara sukarela.

SST yang jelas
MyCA mengira cukai jualan dan cukai perkhidmatan anda daripada buku akaun bagi setiap tempoh dua bulan dan menyusun angka SST-02, sedia untuk anda hantar dan bayar di MySST.

Anggaran cukai pendapatan
Sediakan angka Borang BE atau Borang B anda, berserta pelepasan yang boleh dituntut. Anda memfailkan sendiri di MyTax; MyCA menunjukkan apa yang perlu dimasukkan.

Semua yang diperlukan oleh perniagaan kecil
- Invois, sebut harga, nota kredit dan nota debit
- Rekod jualan, bil dan perbelanjaan dengan menaip satu ayat, atau ambil gambar bil
- Transaksi bank dipadankan dengan buku akaun
- Untung rugi, kunci kira-kira, siapa berhutang dengan anda dan siapa anda berhutang
- Jemput akauntan atau kakitangan anda, masing-masing dengan peranan sendiri
- Eksport semuanya pada bila-bila masa, percuma

Tanya Bahi
Bahi, pembantu MyCA, menjawab soalan tentang e-Invois dan SST berserta sumbernya, dalam bahasa yang mudah.

Pelan (dibilkan setiap bulan melalui Google Play)
- SME: RM299 sebulan
- Growth: RM699 sebulan
- Firm: RM1,999 sebulan, untuk firma perakaunan yang menguruskan ramai pelanggan
Langganan diperbaharui sehingga anda membatalkannya di Google Play.

Data anda
Buku akaun anda disulitkan dan disimpan di Google Cloud di Singapura, dan hanya dihantar melalui HTTPS. Tiada apa yang dihantar kepada LHDN atau sesiapa sehingga anda meluluskannya. Anda boleh memuat turun atau memadam data anda daripada aplikasi.

MyCA tidak bergabung dengan, disokong oleh atau sebahagian daripada LHDN, Jabatan Kastam Diraja Malaysia atau Kerajaan Malaysia, dan bukan ejen cukai. Sumber rasmi: MyTax https://mytax.hasil.gov.my, MyInvois https://myinvois.hasil.gov.my, MySST https://mysst.customs.gov.my.

MyCA dibangunkan oleh ML Dynamic Solutions Private Limited.
```
