# MyCA privacy policy (draft for the owner's review)

Effective date: to be set when published. Controller: the company that publishes
MyCA (to be named here with its registered address and the support email).

## What MyCA is

MyCA is an accounting and compliance app for small businesses. The business owner
enters their own sales, purchases, expenses, payroll and tax details. MyCA keeps those
books and drafts returns from them. It does not file returns, move money or hold
credentials for any government portal.

## What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| Phone number or Google account, name | To sign you in and to show who did what in a business | The hosted service in Google Cloud, Mumbai region |
| The books: invoices, bills, expenses, payroll, tax figures you enter | The service the app provides | The hosted service; on the desktop edition, only on your computer |
| Documents you choose to upload (bank statements, bills, photos of bills) | To import entries or to read a bill into a draft purchase | The hosted service, addressed by a hash of their content |
| Crash reports | To fix crashes; only if you turn them on in Settings | The hosted service, not linked to you |

We do not collect location, contacts, messages, advertising identifiers or anything
from other apps.

## Who else sees it

- Members you invite to your business (staff, your accountant) see what their role allows.
- When the service has a language model switched on, the text of a chat message that
  matches no built-in action, and the text or image of a bill you ask MyCA to read,
  are sent to the model provider to choose an action or copy out the fields. The
  provider is named here when chosen. Figures are never computed by the model.
- When phone sign-in is on, your phone number is sent to the SMS provider named here to
  deliver the sign-in code.
- Nobody else. We do not sell data and we run no advertising or analytics.

## Your rights

You can see everything MyCA holds about your business from inside the app, and download
all of it as one file from the Businesses screen (the books, every uploaded document,
who has access, connected systems and the audit trail). You can delete a business from
the same screen, which removes its books and documents, and delete your account there
too; both take effect at once. Where the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or another law
gives you further rights, you can exercise them through the same address.

## Retention and security

Books are kept while the business exists. Backups of the hosted database are kept for
fourteen days. Data travels over HTTPS and is stored encrypted at rest by the cloud
provider. Sign-in codes expire in five minutes.

## Changes

We will show a new version of this notice inside the app and ask you to accept it
before continuing when anything material changes.
