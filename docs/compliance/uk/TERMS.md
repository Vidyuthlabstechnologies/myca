## 2. What MyCA UK does

MyCA UK is software that helps you:

- keep digital records of your income and expenses, including by importing bank statements or
  connecting a bank feed;
- prepare and send VAT returns under Making Tax Digital for VAT;
- prepare and send quarterly updates for self-employment and UK property income under Making Tax
  Digital for Income Tax, see HMRC's calculations, and send your final declaration;
- estimate your tax, National Insurance, student loan repayments and payments on account;
- check that your UTR, National Insurance number and VAT number are in a valid format;
- get reminders about deadlines and penalty points; and
- ask questions of an in-app assistant.

MyCA UK is built for Making Tax Digital, and HMRC recognition is in progress. [Replace with "MyCA UK
is HMRC recognised software for Making Tax Digital" only after HMRC lists it.] MyCA is not HMRC and
is not part of any government body.

## 3. What MyCA UK does not do

- **It is not tax, legal or financial advice.** The Service gives you tools, calculations and
  general information. If your affairs are complex or you are unsure, ask a qualified accountant or
  tax adviser.
- **We do not act as your agent with HMRC** unless we agree that separately in writing. You (or
  your accountant) remain responsible for what is sent to HMRC in your name.
- **The assistant is software, not a person or an adviser.** It chooses which of MyCA's tools to
  run. Figures come from those tools, not from the assistant, but it can still misunderstand a
  question. Always check the figures on the review screen before you approve anything.
- **Estimates are estimates.** HMRC's own calculation is the one that counts; we show it beside ours
  whenever HMRC provides one.
