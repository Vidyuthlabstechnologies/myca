# MyCA UAE: store listing (Google Play)

Drafted 2026-11-14 for the separate UAE app (`com.mldynamic.myca.uae`, "MyCA UAE"). English
(`en-AE` / `en-US` default) and Arabic (`ar`) listings. The owner and a native Arabic reviewer
approve the wording before anything is submitted; nothing here is published by the repository.
`node scripts/check-listing.mjs docs/uae/PLAY-LISTING.md` checks the fenced fields against Play's
limits (title 30, short description 80, full description 4,000 characters).

What the text may and may not promise (from `docs/uae/COMPLIANCE.md`, sections 4.3, 5 and 8):

- MyCA prepares VAT 201 and Corporate Tax figures. The business or its registered tax agent files
  on EmaraTax. Never "we file", "tax agent", "FTA-approved" or "accredited".
- E-invoicing: "get ready", "PINT AE-ready data". No sending through Peppol and no ASP claim until
  a partner is signed; the app is in sandbox for e-invoicing.
- No prices in the description; Play shows them. No link or hint to pay outside Google Play.
- No competitor names, no "best" or "number one", no ratings or testimonials.
- No government logos in screenshots.

### Full description

```listing-full
MyCA UAE keeps your books and prepares your VAT and Corporate Tax figures, in English or Arabic, so you or your tax agent can file on EmaraTax with confidence.

Your VAT 201, prepared as you go
Record sales and purchases, snap a bill, or import a bank statement. MyCA applies 5% VAT, zero-rated and exempt supplies, and builds your VAT 201 box by box for each quarter. You see every figure's source before you file, and a reminder before the 28-day deadline.

Corporate Tax without the guesswork
MyCA works out taxable income from your books, applies the 0% band up to AED 375,000 and 9% above it, and checks whether Small Business Relief or Qualifying Free Zone Person treatment may apply. Every rule it uses links to the Federal Tax Authority's own guide. Your return is due nine months after your year end; MyCA tracks it for you.

Ready for e-invoicing in 2027
UAE businesses with revenue under AED 50 million must appoint an Accredited Service Provider by 31 March 2027 and issue e-invoices from 1 July 2027. MyCA checks your invoices against the PINT AE data fields today, so your data is clean when you switch on. Sending through Peppol needs an Accredited Service Provider; MyCA will tell you in the app when its provider connection is available.

Arabic and English, side by side
Use the whole app in Arabic with a right-to-left layout, or in English. Tax invoices show both languages, and exports for the FTA can be produced in Arabic.

Snap, ask, approve
Photograph a bill and MyCA reads it into a draft for you to check. Ask the assistant a question in plain words. Nothing is saved, filed or sent until you approve it.

Built for firms too
The Firm plan lets tax agents and accounting firms run many client businesses, each with its own books, roles and audit trail. You file under your own agency on EmaraTax; MyCA is the workbench.

Private by design
Data travels encrypted and is stored encrypted by Google Cloud in Doha, Qatar, until MyCA moves UAE data to a data centre in the UAE. We never sell your data, show ads, or ask for your EmaraTax or UAE Pass password.

Plans
Starter, Business and Firm, billed monthly through Google Play. Cancel any time in Google Play.

Please note
MyCA is software. It is not a registered tax agent and does not file returns or give tax advice. You, or your registered tax agent, review and file your returns on EmaraTax.
```

## Data safety answers

Use `docs/uae/COMPLIANCE.md` section 7.2 verbatim. Summary: collects name, email, phone (optional),
business address and TRN, the books, purchase history, user-picked photos and files, assistant
messages, opt-in crash logs and a random device id; shares nothing for advertising; all
processing is for app functionality and account management; encrypted in transit; deletion in the
app and on the web.
