## English

**MyCA UAE privacy notice**

Effective date: [date of publication]. Version 1.

### Who we are

MyCA UAE is provided by ML Dynamic Solutions Private Limited, a company registered in India,
[registered address] ("MyCA", "we"). [When a UAE entity exists: and by [UAE entity name, licence
number, free zone], which contracts with UAE customers.]

Contact: [privacy email]. Data Protection Officer: [name or service], [email].

### What MyCA is, and what it is not

MyCA UAE is bookkeeping and tax preparation software for businesses in the UAE. You enter or
import your sales, purchases, expenses and bank lines; MyCA keeps your books, prepares your
VAT 201 and Corporate Tax figures and checks your invoices for e-invoicing readiness.

MyCA is not a registered tax agent and does not file returns for you. You, or your registered tax
agent, review and file on the Federal Tax Authority's EmaraTax portal. MyCA never asks for your
EmaraTax or UAE Pass password. MyCA does not move or hold money.

E-invoicing: while MyCA runs in sandbox mode, invoices are checked and shown on your device and
are not sent to anyone. When we connect to an Accredited Service Provider, we will update this
notice and ask you before any invoice is sent.

### Two roles

- For your account (your name, email, phone, plan), MyCA is the **controller**.
- For the contents of your books (your customers', suppliers' and employees' names, Tax
  Registration Numbers, contact details and amounts), your business is the controller and MyCA
  processes that data **on your business's instructions**. If one of your customers or staff asks
  us about their data, we will pass the request to you.

### What we collect and why

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Google account name and email, or phone number | To sign you in and show who did what in a business | Contract with you |
| A random device id our server makes at sign-in | To let you see and sign out your devices | Contract; security |
| Your books: invoices, bills, expenses, payroll, bank lines, VAT and Corporate Tax figures, TRN, trade licence details | The service you asked for | Contract; UAE tax record-keeping law |
| Documents you choose to upload (bank statements, bills, photos of bills) | To import entries or read a bill into a draft | Contract |
| Messages to the assistant | To do what you ask; recent messages are kept for context | Contract |
| Google Play subscription records | To know which plan you have paid for | Contract |
| Crash reports, only if you turn them on (off by default) | To fix crashes. Emails, tokens, TRNs, phone numbers and amounts are removed on your phone before sending | Your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings |
| Marketing emails or WhatsApp messages, only if you opt in | News and tips about MyCA | Your consent, which you can withdraw at any time |

We do not collect your location, contacts, SMS, call logs, advertising identifiers or anything
from other apps. We do not sell your data, run advertising in the app, or use your books to train
AI models.

### Automated suggestions

MyCA's assistant and bill reader suggest categories and entries. A person always confirms an
entry, and you approve every return before you file it. Tax figures are calculated by MyCA's rules
engine, not by a language model. No decision with legal effect is made about you automatically.

### Who else sees your data

Each is reached only when you use the feature, and only with what that feature needs.

- People you invite to your business (staff, your accountant, your tax agent) see what their role
  allows.
- Google: to check a Google sign-in and a Google Play subscription.
- Our hosting provider, Google Cloud, which stores everything above for us (see "Where your data
  is kept").
- The SMS provider, when phone sign-in is on: your phone number and the sign-in code.
- The language model provider, [name, country], when a chat message matches no built-in action or
  you ask MyCA to read a bill: that text or image only.
- The email relay, when you send an email from the app: the message and the addresses.
- An Accredited Service Provider for e-invoicing, [name], only after we connect one and you switch
  it on: the invoice data needed for the Peppol network.
- Authorities, only when UAE law requires it and through due process.

### Where your data is kept

Your data is stored by Google Cloud in **Doha, Qatar** (region `me-central1`), because Google
Cloud has no region in the UAE today. We plan to move UAE data to a data centre in the UAE
[target date, when contracted] and will tell you in the app when we do.

Storing data in Qatar is a transfer outside the UAE. We rely on our contract with Google Cloud,
which requires protection at least equal to UAE law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021,
Article 23), and on the transfer being needed to provide the service you asked for. If the
language model provider is outside the UAE and Qatar, the same contract safeguards apply.

If your business is in the DIFC or ADGM, ask us for our DIFC/ADGM data processing addendum.

### How long we keep it

- Your books are kept while your business is on MyCA.
- UAE law expects businesses to keep tax records for at least 5 years after the tax period ends,
  and 7 years for Corporate Tax and real estate records. When you delete a business, MyCA reminds
  you of this and offers a full download first. The duty to keep records is your business's.
- Database backups are kept for 14 days. Crash reports are kept for 90 days.
- When you delete your account it disappears from the app at once and you are signed out
  everywhere; 30 days later your name, phone number, email and Google account link are erased.
  Signing in again within the 30 days cancels the deletion.

### Your rights

Under Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 you can ask to:

- know what we hold about you and how we use it;
- get a copy, or have it sent to another provider in a readable format (in the app: Businesses,
  Download everything);
- correct it (most of it you can change yourself in the app);
- delete it (in the app: Businesses, Delete my account, or at https://[site]/delete-account);
- restrict or stop processing, including any marketing;
- withdraw consent you gave, without affecting what was done before.

Write to [privacy email]. We answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to
the UAE data protection authority (the UAE Data Office or the authority that replaces it).

### Security

Data travels over HTTPS and is encrypted at rest. Access is limited by role and logged in an audit
trail. Sign-in codes expire in five minutes. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and
the authority, with what happened and what we are doing.

### Children

MyCA UAE is for businesses and people aged 18 and over.

### Changes

When anything material changes, we will show the new version in the app, in English and Arabic,
and ask you to accept it before continuing.

### من يطّلع أيضًا على بياناتك

لا نصل إلى أي جهة إلا عند استخدامك للميزة المعنية، وبالقدر الذي تحتاجه فقط.

- الأشخاص الذين تدعوهم إلى منشأتك (الموظفون، محاسبك، وكيلك الضريبي) يرون ما تسمح به صلاحياتهم.
- Google: للتحقق من تسجيل الدخول عبر Google ومن اشتراك Google Play.
- مزوّد الاستضافة، Google Cloud، الذي يحفظ كل ما سبق نيابةً عنّا (انظر "أين تُحفظ بياناتك").
- مزوّد الرسائل النصية عند تفعيل الدخول برقم الهاتف: رقم هاتفك ورمز الدخول.
- مزوّد النموذج اللغوي، [الاسم، الدولة]، عندما لا تطابق رسالتك أي إجراء مدمج أو تطلب قراءة فاتورة:
  ذلك النص أو تلك الصورة فقط.
- خدمة إرسال البريد، عندما ترسل بريدًا من التطبيق: الرسالة والعناوين.
- مزوّد خدمة معتمد للفوترة الإلكترونية، [الاسم]، فقط بعد ربطه وتفعيلك له: بيانات الفاتورة اللازمة
  لشبكة Peppol.
- الجهات الرسمية، فقط عندما يوجب قانون الإمارات ذلك ووفق الإجراءات النظامية.

### أين تُحفظ بياناتك

تُحفظ بياناتك لدى Google Cloud في **الدوحة، قطر** (المنطقة `me-central1`)، لأن Google Cloud لا تملك
منطقة داخل الإمارات حاليًا. ونعتزم نقل بيانات عملاء الإمارات إلى مركز بيانات داخل الدولة [الموعد
المستهدف عند التعاقد]، وسنبلغك داخل التطبيق عند ذلك.

يُعدّ حفظ البيانات في قطر نقلًا لها خارج الدولة. ونستند في ذلك إلى عقدنا مع Google Cloud الذي يُلزمها
بحماية لا تقل عن قانون الإمارات (المرسوم بقانون اتحادي رقم 45 لسنة 2021، المادة 23)، وإلى أن النقل
لازم لتقديم الخدمة التي طلبتها. وإذا كان مزوّد النموذج اللغوي خارج الإمارات وقطر، تسري الضمانات
التعاقدية نفسها.

إذا كانت منشأتك في مركز دبي المالي العالمي (DIFC) أو سوق أبوظبي العالمي (ADGM)، اطلب منّا ملحق معالجة
البيانات الخاص بهما.
