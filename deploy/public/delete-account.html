Delete your MyCA account

Draft. The support email and the company name are filled in by the owner before this page is published.

From the app

  1. Open MyCA and sign in.
  2. Open Businesses from the menu.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

You are signed out on every device straight away. A business where you are the only owner is deleted with your account. A business that has another owner stays with them, and you are removed from it.

Without the app

Write to the support email from the phone number's owner or the Google account you sign in with, and say "Delete my MyCA account". We confirm it is you and delete the account within thirty days.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your name, phone number or Google account link, and your devicesDeleted
The books, documents and chat of a business you alone ownDeleted
Crash reportsNot linked to you; deleted on their usual schedule
Records the law requires a business to keep, such as tax invoices and the audit trail of filingsKept only for the period the law sets, then deleted. They are not used for anything else
