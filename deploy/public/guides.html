Guides

Plain-language guides to GST billing, GST returns, e-invoicing, moving from Tally, CA firm workflows and US federal returns, from the makers of MyCA.

GST and books in India

  • GST billing that keeps your books while you bill.

    Make GST invoices on your phone by typing a sentence. MyCA works out CGST, SGST or IGST, keeps the books and builds GSTR-1 from them. 14 days of Pro to start.

  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, already worked out.

    MyCA builds GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B from the invoices and bills already in your books, shows how each figure was reached, and files on the GST portal with your approval.

  • E-invoices without the JSON.

    Create GST e-invoices from your phone. MyCA prepares the e-invoice from your sale, gets the IRN and signed QR code, and keeps them with the invoice in your books.

  • Keep your Tally history. Lose the desk.

    Move from Tally to MyCA without losing history: bring in your Tally export, keep billing on your phone, and send your CA a Tally file whenever they ask.

  • Your clients keep the books. You approve.

    Give every client books they keep themselves on the phone, and approve their GST filings from one place. MyCA CA firm plan: 25 people across the firm.

US federal taxes

  • Your federal return, with every line explained.

    Prepare your 2026 federal return free on your phone and see your refund first. Every line explained. Pay only when you file: $99 through April 15, 2027.

  • Freelance income, without the guesswork.

    Freelancers, contractors and gig workers: MyCA US prepares Schedule C, Schedule SE and the QBI deduction from your 1099-NEC and expenses, and explains every figure.
