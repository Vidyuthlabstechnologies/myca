One sale, all the way through

From one invoice to a filed return

The tax invoice is the source of truth. Your ledger, your GST returns and your CA's export are all worked out from it, so they always agree.

  1. Invoice

    Shared on WhatsApp as a PDF, with the e-invoice IRN when you connect NIC.

  2. Books

    Sales, GST payable and the customer's balance update together.

  3. Returns

    GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B are drafted from the invoices, reviewed step by step.

  4. Filed

    Through a licensed GST Suvidha Provider, after your CA approves.

Security

Security and privacy, in plain words

Your books are yours

On the phone they live in our service in Mumbai, one business to one record, and nobody who is not a member of your business can see them. On Windows they never leave your computer.

Nothing happens without you

Every entry is previewed and confirmed. Reminders go from your own phone. Filing a return needs the live switch and a CA's approval, and MyCA never holds your portal password.

The app holds no tax logic

The phone app sends sentences and shows answers; the figures are worked out on the server from dated rules you can read. There is nothing on the phone worth taking apart.

Every figure can be traced

To the invoice behind it, the rule it used, the section of the Act and the date the rule took effect. Each figure shows whether the rule behind it has been independently reviewed yet.

You can leave with everything

A full export, a Tally file and the return files are free on every plan, and deleting your account is a button.

Nothing hidden

The full notice is in our privacy policy. What is still open before launch is written down in the repository's security document.

Questions

Questions owners ask

Is MyCA GST billing software?

Yes. MyCA makes GST tax invoices and quotes as PDFs you can share on WhatsApp, with the CGST, SGST or IGST split worked out from the place of supply, and keeps your books from the same invoices.

Can MyCA file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B?

MyCA works out GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B from your invoices and bills on every plan. On Pro it also files them on the GST portal through a licensed GST Suvidha Provider, after you or your CA approve.

Do I need to know accounting to use it?

No. You say what happened in your own words. MyCA shows the entry it is about to make, asks when something is missing, and posts nothing until you say yes.

Can I move from Tally or Zoho Books?

Yes. MyCA reads a Tally export, a Zoho Books file or a bank statement, or you can start fresh. Your CA can still get a Tally file from MyCA whenever they want one.

Where are my books kept?

With the phone app, in our service in Mumbai, one business to one record. With the Windows app, on your own computer, with a daily backup.

Is there a free trial?

Every plan starts with 14 days of Pro. If you do not pick a plan after that, nothing is deleted: your books stay readable and exportable.

Does MyCA replace my CA?

No. MyCA is software, not a firm of chartered accountants, and it does not give tax or legal advice. Your CA can work in the same books and approve returns before they are filed.
