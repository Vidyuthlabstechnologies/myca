Three steps, every time

Sales, bills, expenses, receipts, payroll and returns all work the same way, so there is nothing to learn twice.

1Say itIn your words, in English or Hindi: "paid 8,000 rent", "received 5,000 from Kiran Stores", "who owes me?". Or fill a short form, or photograph a bill.
2See itMyCA shows exactly what it will record: the amounts, the GST split, the due date, what changes in your books and in your returns. If something is missing, it asks instead of guessing.
3Say yesOnly then is it posted. Every figure is traced to the invoice behind it and the rule it used, so your CA can check it in a minute.

Security and privacy, in plain words

Your books are yours. On the phone they live in our service in Mumbai, one business to one record, and nobody who is not a member of your business can see them. On Windows they never leave your computer.

Nothing is posted, sent or filed without you. Every entry is previewed and confirmed. Reminders go from your own phone. Filing a return needs the live switch and a CA's approval, and MyCA never holds your portal password.

The app holds no tax logic. The phone app sends sentences and shows answers; the figures are worked out on the server from dated rules you can read. There is nothing on the phone worth taking apart.

Every figure can be traced. To the invoice behind it, the rule it used, the section of the Act and the date the rule took effect. If a rule has not been checked by our partner CA firm yet, the figure says so.

You can leave with everything. A full export, a Tally file and the return files are free on every plan, and deleting your account is a button.

The full notice is at /privacy.html. What is still open before launch is written down in the repository's security document, not hidden.
