How MyCA does it

The tax split is worked out for you

CGST and SGST inside your state, IGST across states, from the place of supply on the invoice. Rates come from dated rules you can read.

Invoices you can share at once

Send the PDF on WhatsApp from your phone, and remind the customer from MyCA when it falls due.

Every invoice feeds the return

Sales flow into GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B. There is nothing to re-type at month end.

Your CA sees the same books

Invite your accountant. They work in the same records, and every change shows who made it.

Questions

Is MyCA free?

No. MyCA starts with 14 days of Pro. After that Essentials is ₹399 a month and Pro is ₹799 a month, with a discount by the year. If you do not pick a plan, your books stay readable and exportable.

Can I make e-invoices?

Yes, on Pro. MyCA prepares the e-invoice and gets the IRN when you confirm.

Can I bring my old invoices from Tally?

Yes. MyCA reads a Tally export, a Zoho Books file or a bank statement, and you can start fresh instead.
