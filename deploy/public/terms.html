1. What MyCA is, and what it is not

MyCA is accounting and compliance software for small businesses in India. You enter your own sales, purchases, expenses, payroll and tax details; MyCA keeps your books, works out figures from the rules it shows you, drafts returns and, only when you confirm, sends them through a licensed GST Suvidha Provider. MyCA is not a firm of chartered accountants, does not practise accountancy and does not give tax, legal or investment advice. MyCA is not affiliated with or approved by GSTN, the Income Tax Department or NIC.

2. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the accuracy of what you enter, for checking every draft before you approve it, and for every return, invoice, e-invoice and e-way bill filed or generated on your approval. Keep your sign-in and the OTPs sent to you private. Tax law asks you, the taxpayer, to keep your records; MyCA lets you download all of them at any time, free.

3. Accounts and age

You must be 18 or over and able to enter a contract. If you use MyCA for a business, you confirm you are allowed to act for it. You decide who you invite to a business and what role they have.

4. Plans, trial and payment

Every new business gets a 14-day free trial. Paid plans are monthly subscriptions bought through Google Play, renew automatically each month until you cancel, and are billed by Google. Prices are shown before you subscribe, with the tax that applies. When a trial or plan ends, your books become read-only; you can still see, ask and export them. See Refunds and cancellation.

5. MyCA Assured

MyCA Assured opens only once an independent GST practitioner or chartered accountant is named in the app, with their membership or enrolment number, before you subscribe. That professional is engaged by you, reviews and files your returns, and is responsible for their own professional work under the rules of their institute. MyCA provides the software and does not share in their professional fees.

6. Correction promise

If a return filed through MyCA is wrong because of an error in MyCA's software (not because of the data entered or a change you approved), tell us within 90 days of filing. We will prepare the correcting entries or amended return at no charge. Interest, late fees, penalties and the cost of any professional you engage are not covered.

7. Acceptable use

Do not use MyCA to make false invoices or returns, to claim credit you are not entitled to, to break the law, or to try to reach another person's data. We may suspend an account that does, and we will tell you why unless the law prevents it.

8. Your data

Your books belong to you. How we handle personal data is in the privacy policy. You can delete a business or your account at any time.

9. Availability and changes

We work to keep MyCA available but cannot promise it will never be interrupted, for example when a government portal or provider is down. Due dates remain yours to meet; file early when a portal is busy. We will tell you in the app before a material change to these terms takes effect.
