Free trial

A new business gets 14 days free. No card is needed and nothing is charged when the trial ends; the books simply become read-only until you choose a plan.

Cancelling a plan

Plans are monthly subscriptions bought through Google Play and renew automatically until you cancel. Cancel any time in the Google Play app: Profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, MyCA, Cancel. The app links straight there from Plan and billing. Your plan keeps working until the end of the month you have paid for, and is not renewed after that.

Refunds

Google Play takes the payment, so refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy: you can ask Google for a refund from your Google Play order history. If you were charged in error, for a plan that did not work, or for a month after you cancelled, write to [support email] with the order number and we will ask Google to refund you or refund you ourselves.

MyCA Assured

The fee covers the software. The independent professional's fee, if they charge you separately, is between you and them.

Your data after cancelling

Cancelling does not delete anything. You can still see and download all your books. To erase them, delete the business or your account in the app.
