Delete your MyCA US account

Draft for legal review. The bracketed details are filled in by the owner before this page is published.

This page is for the MyCA US app by Quanzo AI. Support: [support email].

From the app

  1. Open MyCA US and sign in.
  2. Tap the account icon at the top right of your return.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

You are signed out on every device straight away. Thirty days later your returns, tax documents, saved Social Security numbers, name, email and sign-in link are erased for good; signing in again before then cancels the deletion. If you pay for MyCA Plus through Google Play or the App Store, cancel it there too, because deleting your account does not stop the store billing you.

Without the app

Write to [support email] from the email address of the Google or Apple account you sign in with, or say which phone number you use, and ask us to delete your MyCA US account. We confirm it is you and delete the account within thirty days. Someone you authorize can ask for you.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your returns, tax forms, answers, Social Security numbers and ITINs, bank details and documentsErased thirty days after you ask
Your name, email, phone number, sign-in link and devicesErased thirty days after you ask
A return MyCA itself sent to the IRS, with its signed Form 8879Kept for three years from the filing due date or the date the IRS received it, whichever is later, as the IRS requires, then erased. Not used for anything else
A return our e-file partner filedKept by the partner under its own policy; ask the partner to delete what it holds
Payment recordsKept by Stripe, Google Play or Apple as the law requires them to; MyCA keeps only that a return was paid for
Crash reportsNot linked to you; deleted on their usual schedule

Backups are overwritten within two weeks. Read the privacy notice for the rest.
