Preparing your return is free

You can add your forms, answer the questions, see your refund or amount due, review every line and download the review copy without paying anything. You pay only when you choose to file.

The filing fee

Filing one federal return for one tax year costs $129. For the 2027 filing season the launch price is $99 if you pay by 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on April 15, 2027. The price is shown before you pay, and the fee covers one federal return for one tax year. State returns MyCA prepares are filed with your federal return through our e-file partner [state filing price to be set before release]. Card payments are processed by Stripe; MyCA never sees your full card number.

When we refund the filing fee

  • Before you file. If you paid but have not sent your return yet, write to [support email] and we refund the fee in full.
  • Charged twice. If you were charged more than once for the same return and year, MyCA refunds the extra charges in full automatically, as soon as the second payment comes through. If a refund has not reached your card within 10 business days, write to [support email].
  • Return we cannot file. If your return is rejected and cannot be corrected and filed again through MyCA, we refund the fee in full.
  • Our mistake. See the accuracy promise below.

Once your return has been sent and accepted, the fee is not otherwise refundable, because the service has been delivered. Refunds go back to the card you paid with, usually within 5 to 10 business days. Disputing a charge with your bank does not delete your return or your account.

Accuracy promise

If a mistake in MyCA's calculations means your federal return needs to be corrected, we fix it and you file the correction at no extra charge. If the IRS has already accepted the return, the correction is an amended return (Form 1040-X). MyCA prepares it for you to print, sign and mail, because it cannot e-file amended returns yet, and we refund your MyCA filing fee in full. This promise does not cover IRS penalties or interest, mistakes in information you entered, or changes in law after you filed.

MyCA Plus

MyCA Plus is a yearly subscription, $249 a year, bought in the app by card (through Stripe), through Google Play or through the App Store. It renews automatically every year at $249 until you cancel. You can cancel any time: a card subscription in the app (Account, MyCA Plus, Cancel), a Google Play one in Google Play (Profile, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions), and an App Store one on iPhone (Settings, your name, Subscriptions). Plus keeps working until the end of the year you paid for and is not renewed after that. Before a yearly card renewal we email you the date and the price [Stripe upcoming-renewal emails to be switched on before release]. Refunds for Plus bought through Google Play or Apple follow their refund policy; for a card subscription, or if you were charged in error, write to [support email] and we will help.

Expert Review

Expert Review is not sold yet. Joining the waitlist is free and costs nothing later unless you choose to buy it.

Your data after you stop

Stopping or cancelling does not delete anything. You can still see and download your return. To erase it, delete your account in the app or at Delete your account. A return MyCA has filed with the IRS itself is kept for three years, as the IRS requires, and then erased.
