Self-employed

Freelance income, without the guesswork.

Add your 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC forms and your business expenses. MyCA prepares Schedule C, works out self-employment tax on Schedule SE and applies the qualified business income deduction.

How MyCA US does it

Schedule C from your records

Income from 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC, and your expenses on the right Schedule C lines, each with the IRS instruction behind it.

Self-employment tax worked out

Schedule SE, with half of it deducted on Schedule 1 automatically.

QBI deduction applied

Form 8995 for the 20% qualified business income deduction where you qualify.

Year-round with Plus

MyCA Plus ($249 a year) works out quarterly estimated payments, checks your withholding and reads IRS letters.

Questions

Do I need a separate business account?

No, but separating business expenses makes Schedule C easier, and MyCA can read a bank feed for them.

Are quarterly estimated payments included?

MyCA Plus works out each quarterly payment and shows the due dates.
