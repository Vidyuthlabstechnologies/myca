How MyCA does it

Built from what you recorded

Sales go to GSTR-1 tables, bills and expenses give input tax credit for GSTR-3B. Nothing is typed twice.

Checked against GSTR-2B

MyCA matches your purchase bills to what suppliers reported and shows what is missing before you claim it.

Filed only when you say so

On Pro, MyCA files on the GST portal after you, or your CA, approve the figures. It never holds your portal password.

Due dates on your home screen

MyCA reminds you before the 11th and the 20th, and on the phone itself, not by a call centre.

Questions

Does MyCA file the return for me?

On Pro it can, after you or your CA approve. On Essentials MyCA works out the return and you file it yourself.

What if a supplier has not filed?

The GSTR-2B check marks the bill as not yet reported, so you can hold the credit and remind the supplier.

Can my CA review before filing?

Yes. Invite your CA as an accountant. Filing waits for their approval when you ask it to.
