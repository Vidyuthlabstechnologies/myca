MyCA privacy policy

Draft. The effective date, the company name and address, the support email, the grievance officer and the named processors are filled in by the owner before this page is published.

What MyCA is

MyCA is an accounting and compliance app for small businesses. You enter your own sales, purchases, expenses, payroll and tax details. MyCA keeps those books and drafts returns from them. It does not file returns, move money or hold credentials for any government portal.

What we collect and why

DataWhyWhere it is kept
Phone number or Google account, nameTo sign you in and to show who did what in a businessThe hosted service in Google Cloud, Mumbai region
Your books: invoices, bills, expenses, payroll, tax figures you enterThe service the app providesThe hosted service
Documents you choose to uploadTo import entries or to read a bill into a draft purchaseThe hosted service, addressed by a hash of their content
Crash reportsTo fix crashes; only if you turn them onThe hosted service, not linked to you

We do not collect location, contacts, messages, advertising identifiers or anything from other apps.

Who else sees it

  • Members you invite to your business see what their role allows.
  • When a language model is switched on, the text of a chat message that matches no built-in action, and the text or image of a bill you ask MyCA to read, go to the model provider to choose an action or copy out the fields. Figures are never computed by the model.
  • When phone sign-in is on, your phone number goes to the SMS provider to deliver the sign-in code.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell data and we run no advertising or analytics.

Your rights

You can see everything MyCA holds about your business inside the app. You can delete a business from the Businesses screen. You can delete your account yourself from the Businesses screen, or ask for it by writing to the support email. You are signed out at once, and thirty days later your name, phone number, email and Google account link are erased for good; signing in again before then cancels it. The steps are on the account deletion page. Rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 can be exercised through the same address.

Retention and security

Books are kept while the business exists. Backups are kept for fourteen days. Data travels over HTTPS and is stored encrypted at rest. Sign-in codes expire in five minutes.

Changes

A new version of this notice is shown inside the app and must be accepted before continuing when anything material changes.
