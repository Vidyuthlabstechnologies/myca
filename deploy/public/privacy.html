What MyCA is

MyCA is an accounting and compliance app for small businesses. You enter your own sales, purchases, expenses, payroll and tax details. MyCA keeps those books, drafts returns from them and, only when you confirm with the OTP the government portal sends, files GST returns through a licensed GST Suvidha Provider. MyCA does not move or hold money and never asks for or stores a password for a government portal.

Who else sees it

Each of these is reached only when you use the feature, and only with what that feature needs.

  • Members you invite to your business see what their role allows.
  • Google, to check a Google sign-in and a Google Play subscription.
  • The SMS provider, when phone sign-in is on: your phone number and the sign-in code.
  • The GST Suvidha Provider (currently Quicko), when you file a GST return or use the e-way bill service through it: the return data, your GSTIN and the OTP step.
  • The NIC e-invoice and e-way bill systems, when you generate an IRN or e-way bill: the invoice data.
  • The language model provider, when a model is switched on: the text of a chat message that matches no built-in action, and the text or image of a bill you ask MyCA to read. Figures are never computed by the model.
  • The WhatsApp gateway, when you send a reminder or link WhatsApp: the message and the recipient's number.
  • The email relay, when you send an email from the app: the message and the addresses.
  • Connected services you switch on yourself (a marketplace, payment or payroll system): what that connection syncs.
  • Kit, our email list provider in the United States, if you join the MyCA US early-access list on this website: your email address, used only to tell you about the MyCA US launch. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Our hosting provider, Google Cloud, which stores everything above on our behalf.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell data, we run no advertising and we use no third-party analytics.

Where it is kept, and transfers outside India

Your account and books are stored on Google Cloud in Mumbai, India, and our logs are kept in India. Two features can send information outside India, only when you use them: the language model provider, which may process a chat message or a bill in the United States, and the email relay, which runs on a worldwide network. We do not transfer personal data to any country the Government of India has restricted.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask what we hold and who we shared it with, have it corrected, completed or erased, withdraw your consent, nominate someone to act for you if you die or cannot act yourself, and complain. In the app: Your businesses, Your privacy rights; every request gets a reference number, an acknowledgement within 48 hours and an answer within 30 days. Or write to the Grievance Officer. You can also download everything MyCA holds about a business from the Businesses screen, delete a business there, and delete your account there or through the account deletion page. Deleting your account withdraws your consent: you are signed out at once, and thirty days later your name, phone number, email and Google account link are erased for good; signing in again before then cancels it. If you are not satisfied with our answer you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

MyCA is for people aged 18 and over who keep books for a business. Everyone confirms they are 18 or over before using it.

Retention and security

Books are kept while the business exists and erased thirty days after you delete it. Backups are kept for fourteen days. Security logs and the record of who looked at your books are kept for one year, as the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 require. Tax law asks you, the taxpayer, to keep your records for six years or more, so download a copy before deleting. Data travels over HTTPS and is stored encrypted at rest; the books are sealed again with a separate key. Sign-in codes expire in five minutes.

If there is a breach

If your personal data is exposed in a breach we will tell you without delay, in the app and by email where we have it, with what happened, when, what it means for you, what we are doing and what you can do, and we will report it to the Data Protection Board of India and to CERT-In within the times the law sets.

Changes

A new version of this notice is shown inside the app and must be accepted before continuing when anything material changes.
