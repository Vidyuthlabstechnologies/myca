What MyCA is

MyCA is an accounting and compliance app for small businesses. You enter your own sales, purchases, expenses, payroll and tax details. MyCA keeps those books, drafts returns from them and, only when you confirm with the OTP the government portal sends, files GST returns through a licensed GST Suvidha Provider. MyCA does not move or hold money and never asks for or stores a password for a government portal.

Who else sees it

Each of these is reached only when you use the feature, and only with what that feature needs.

  • Members you invite to your business see what their role allows.
  • Google, to check a Google sign-in and a Google Play subscription.
  • The SMS provider, when phone sign-in is on: your phone number and the sign-in code.
  • The GST Suvidha Provider (currently Quicko), when you file a GST return or use the e-way bill service through it: the return data, your GSTIN and the OTP step.
  • The NIC e-invoice and e-way bill systems, when you generate an IRN or e-way bill: the invoice data.
  • The language model provider, when a model is switched on: the text of a chat message that matches no built-in action, and the text or image of a bill you ask MyCA to read. Figures are never computed by the model.
  • The WhatsApp gateway, when you send a reminder or link WhatsApp: the message and the recipient's number.
  • The email relay, when you send an email from the app: the message and the addresses.
  • Connected services you switch on yourself (a marketplace, payment or payroll system): what that connection syncs.
  • Our hosting provider, Google Cloud, which stores everything above on our behalf.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell data and we run no advertising or analytics.

Your rights

You can see everything MyCA holds about your business inside the app and download all of it as one file from the Businesses screen. You can delete a business from the same screen, and delete your account there too, or follow the account deletion page without the app. You are signed out at once, and thirty days later your name, phone number, email and Google account link are erased for good; signing in again before then cancels it. Records the law makes us keep are kept only as long as it requires. Your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including correction, erasure, grievance redressal and nominating someone to act for you, can be exercised by writing to the support email or the Grievance Officer.

Children

MyCA is for business owners aged 18 and over and is not meant for children.

Retention and security

Books are kept while the business exists. Backups are kept for fourteen days. Data travels over HTTPS and is stored encrypted at rest. Sign-in codes expire in five minutes.

Changes

A new version of this notice is shown inside the app and must be accepted before continuing when anything material changes.
