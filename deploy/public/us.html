Questions

When can I file?

MyCA US opens for the 2026 tax year in January 2027.

Does it do state returns?

Not this season. MyCA gives you a carry-over sheet with the federal figures your state return needs.

What if MyCA makes a mistake?

If a mistake in MyCA's calculations means your federal return needs to be corrected, we fix it and you file the correction at no extra charge. This promise does not cover IRS penalties or interest.

Can a tax professional review my return?

Expert Review is coming soon, and you can join the waitlist in the app. Until then you can share your return with your own tax professional from the app.

Is MyCA connected to the IRS?

No. MyCA is not affiliated with the IRS or any government agency. Your return is e-filed through our IRS-authorized e-file partner.
