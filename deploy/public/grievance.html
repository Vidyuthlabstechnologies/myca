How to raise a grievance

In the app: Your businesses, Your privacy rights, Make a complaint. Every complaint gets a reference number at once. Or write to the email above with your registered phone number or email and what went wrong.

When we answer

We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within one month of receiving it. You can see the status and our answer in the app under Your privacy rights.

Your privacy rights

From the same screen you can ask what personal data we hold and who it was shared with, have it corrected, completed or erased, withdraw your consent, or nominate someone to act for you if you die or cannot act yourself.

If you are not satisfied

You can complain to the Data Protection Board of India about how your personal data was handled, or approach the consumer commission for a consumer dispute.
